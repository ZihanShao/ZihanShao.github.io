## About

I am a senior undergraduate student in **Mathematics at Nanjing University** and currently a visiting researcher at **Yale University**, where I work on LLM reasoning.

My research interests lie in **LLM reasoning and interpretability**, particularly **latent reasoning, reasoning verification, and test-time computation**. I am interested in understanding how internal reasoning states evolve and when additional computation corresponds to meaningful reasoning progress.

## Research

### Recurrent Latent Reasoning
**Yale University · 2026–Present**

I study recurrent latent reasoning in pretrained language models, with a focus on intermediate-state supervision, reasoning across computation depths, and the semantics of continuous latent reasoning states.

### Neuro-Symbolic Reasoning
**Nanjing University · 2024–2026**

I worked on differentiable reasoning with fuzzy **ALC description logic**, studying how symbolic constraints and fuzzy semantics affect neural reasoning behavior.

This work resulted in a first-author **oral paper at ACM SIGKDD 2026**.
